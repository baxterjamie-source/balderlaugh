How to play

Each round
✍️
Bluff. You get a strange word, person or movie. Write a fake answer that sounds real.
📖
Listen. One player reads all the answers out loud. The real one is hiding in there.
🗳️
Vote. Tap Real on the one you think is true. Tap 😂 on the funniest.
😂
Laugh. See the real answer, who wrote what, and who got fooled.
Points
✅
+2for picking the real answer
+1for every player your bluff fools
😂
+1for every funniest vote (if the host turns it on)
Good to know
🚫
You can't vote for your own answer.
👀
No peeking at other apps. If you leave the game mid-round, everyone finds out.
🪑
Need a break? Tap ⋯ and choose Sit out.
🤖
The host can let Claude play. Claude writes a bluff and can score, but never votes for the real answer.
